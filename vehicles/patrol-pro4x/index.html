MYNISSAN

MYNISSAN

The MyNISSAN App offers various ownership services at your convenience. It is constantly upgraded to meet your evolving needs as our customer. Start by creating your unique NissanConnect ID and connect your whole Nissan experience via the app. With the multi-user feature, your family and friends can be a part of this experience. You can create up to 3 profiles (Owner, Standard, Lite) for additional benefits, such as 'Plan my Journey' and remote vehicle status check.

1/3 Remote Door Control & Engine Start

Use the MyNISSAN App to remotely lock or unlock your car’s doors from any location. Avoid the heat by using the remote engine start to turn on the air conditioning.

2/3 My Car Finder

Your Nissan Patrol is always within reach with the My Car Finder feature. Use the MyNISSAN App, and it will tell you the exact location from when the ignition was last turned off.

3/3 Remote Climate Control

Once you have remotely activated the cooler monitor, adjust the temperature of the interior to ensure it is perfectly set for when you want to start your journey.
