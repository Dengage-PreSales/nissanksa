INTELLIGENCE THAT'S STREETS AHEAD

Enjoy the drive even more. With the groundbreaking ProPILOT, your New Nissan Altima helps keep you centered in your lane, and following the car ahead at a preset distance. It can even bring your New Nissan Altima to a full stop based on the highway traffic flow and can bring you back up to speed when traffic starts moving again.

EXTERIOR FEATURES

The New Nissan Altima is an eye-catching masterpiece with its sleek and stylish exterior design. Featuring a reimagined v-motion front grille that exudes power and confidence, equipped with high output LED headlights with integrated DRLs. An aerodynamic rear design ensuring that every detail is carefully crafted with precision. With a turbine design 19” alloy wheel and a Sport grade package, the New Altima is all about style and performance.

1/3 Reimagined V-Motion Front Grille

2/3 Sleek and Aerodynamic Rear Design

3/3 Turbine Design 19” Alloy Wheels

INTERIOR FEATURES

From the premium leather-appointed seats to the advanced technology at your fingertips, every detail has been crafted with your comfort in mind. The centrepiece of the interior is the all-new 12.3" Display screen with multi-touch control, providing seamless and wireless connectivity with Apple CarPlay™ and Android Auto™. And with Bose Premium Audio® and active noise cancellation, you can enjoy your favourite tunes without any distractions.

1/6 Premium Leather-appointed Seats

2/6 12.3” HD Color Display with Multi-Touch Control

3/6 7” Advanced Driver-Assist Display

4/6 Bose Premium Audio® with 9 Speakers and Active Noise Cancellation

5/6 Wireless Apple CarPlay™ & Wireless Android Auto™

6/6 Wireless Charging

NO ONE HAS DONE POWER AND EFFICIENCY LIKE THIS BEFORE

Get the most efficiency out of your New Nissan Altima with the all-new 4-cylinder Direct Injection Gasoline engine. Standard on every New Nissan Altima, it produces an impressive 16.6 - 17.6 KMPL combined, while running quicker, quieter, and smoother than previous Altima engines.
