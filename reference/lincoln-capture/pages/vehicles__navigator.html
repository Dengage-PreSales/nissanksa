FAQs

 

What is the price of the Lincoln Navigator in KSA?

The price of the Lincoln Navigator in Saudi Arabia varies depending on the selected trim and optional features. For exact pricing, customers can contact their nearest Lincoln showroom.

What are the key features of the Lincoln Navigator?

Key features include advanced driver-assist technologies, a spacious luxury interior, premium leather seating, a powerful twin-turbocharged engine, large digital displays, and a smooth, quiet driving experience.

How does the Lincoln Navigator compare to other luxury SUVs?

The Navigator stands out with its exceptional cabin space, strong towing capability, refined ride quality, advanced technology, and premium materials — making it one of the top options in the full-size luxury SUV segment.

Where can I buy a Lincoln Navigator in Saudi Arabia?

The Lincoln Navigator is available through Lincoln Al Naghi authorized showrooms across Saudi Arabia. Customers can visit the nearest branch to explore models or schedule a test drive.

What engine options are available for the Lincoln Navigator?

The Navigator comes with a powerful twin-turbocharged engine engineered to deliver smooth acceleration, impressive torque, and refined performance suited for both city driving and long-distance travel.

Does the Lincoln Navigator come with advanced safety features?

Yes. The Navigator includes a comprehensive suite of advanced safety and driver-assistance features such as collision warning, adaptive cruise control, lane-keeping systems, and more.
