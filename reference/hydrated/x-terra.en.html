REDESIGNED EXTERIOR WITH UPGRADED INTERIOR

The new X-Terra was designed to reflect first-class refinement and a free spirit. Sleek, modern lines on top of a wide stance tell the world that you’re ready for anything.

COLORS & GALLERY

FRONT EXTERIOR FEATURES

1/3 Redesigned Front Fascia with V-Motion Grille

X-Terra features an upscale new look up front with sleek lines and a large V-Motion Grille.

2/3 Quad LED Headlights with Signature

X-Terra’s LED lighting lets you see and be seen. Unique quad LED lights create a signature look with premium style and extend your nighttime view of what’s ahead further than before.

3/3 Fender Accents

X-Terra’s chrome fender accents bridge its modern design and rugged off-road capability.

REAR EXTERIOR FEATURES

1/3 Redesigned Rear Fascia

You’ll look good coming and going. X-Terra’s redesigned rear fascia features an eye-catching and sporty look, a large chrome license finisher and a wide stance.

2/3 18” Machine-Finished Wheels

X-Terra features 18” machine-finished aluminium-alloy wheels that are lightweight, strong, and stylish.

3/3 LED Tail Lamps

New rear LED tail lamps add a crisp modern design and accent the updated rear fascia.

PREMIUM INTERIOR & COMFORT

Get used to the finer things, with premium interior curated for everyone's comfort.

INTERIOR COLORS

EVERY SEAT IS FIRST-CLASS

Get used to the finer things, with an interior curated for everyone. Think plush leather-appointed seats, individual climate controls, and low-fatigue Zero Gravity Seats.

1/4 QUIET INTERIOR

X-Terra features sound-dampening acoustic glass that helps keep road noise out of your interior, so you can better focus on what’s important to you.

2/4 DUAL ZONE AUTOMATIC TEMPERATURE CONTROL

Dual Zone Automatic Temperature Control lets you and your front passenger set and enjoy your own temp.

3/4 REAR AIR CONDITIONING

Dial in the comfort with ceiling-mounted second- and third-row climate controls.

4/4 TAKE A SEAT IN THE EXECUTIVE SUITE

Keep a handle on your projects from your new corner office. Take meetings in the whisper-quiet interior, and always stay cool – thanks to rear AC with 360° vents and stylish ambient lighting. It's so accommodating, you might decide to work remotely today.

PREMIUM INTERIOR SEATING

1/4 SECOND-ROW SEAT RELEASE

3rd row entry and exit is easy thanks to side steps and a second-row that tips forward with a flip of a switch or the pull of a lever.

2/4 ZERO GRAVITY SEATS

Zero Gravity seats slide and recline to bring ultimate comfort and legroom. Reducing fatigue on long drives, Zero Gravity seats keep front and second-row passengers comfy.

3/4 THEATRE-STYLE REAR SEATING

With ascending second and third rows, everyone has a clear view of the road ahead or the 11” flip-down monitor.

4/4 FOLD-DOWN REAR ARMREST WITH CUP HOLDERS

Add a little extra comfort and storage. Drop down the second-row centre armrest to increase the number of usable cup holders to 10.

UPGRADED INTERIOR

X-Terra’s interior is filled with impressive details that invite you into the VIP suite. From leather appointments to premium comfort features, X-Terra keeps you riding in style.

X-Terra’s dash has been redesigned to include a large 9” touch-screen display and sleek, sweeping lines.

2/3 9” Touch-Screen Display

The largest touch-screen display ever found in an X-Terra lets you stay connected to your smartphone and your wider world.

3/3 Advanced Drive-Assist Display

Safety features, directions, fuel economy, and more – you’ll enjoy a wide variety of screens on the full-colour 7” Advanced Drive-Assist Display.

SPACIOUS INTERIOR

1/3 Upgraded Touchpoints

From the leather-appointed seats to the chrome accents, X-Terra was designed with upscale looks and touchpoints throughout.

2/3 Cargo Versatility

Take on adventures big and small with flexible cargo space that features a 60/40 split-folding second row and a 50/50 split third row.

3/3 Seating for Seven

Seat 7 comfortably in a roomy interior packed with creature comforts for every passenger. Whether it’s leather-appointed seats or climate control in each row, you’ll keep everyone happy.

CONNECTIVITY FEATURES

1/3 NISSAN NAVIGATION SYSTEM

In a navigation-equipped Nissan, a GPS system is built right into your dashboard. Stay focused on the road ahead as the system guides you with visual and voice commands.

2/3 WIRELESS APPLE CARPLAY INTEGRATION

People to see? Places to be? Just ask Siri. Equipped with Siri® voice control, wireless Apple CarPlay brings your contacts, favorite messaging apps, Apple Music playlists, Apple Maps, and more on board to your X-Terra. Simply sync your compatible iPhone and go.

3/3 ANDROID AUTO

With Android Auto, you can keep your eyes on the road and hands on the wheel while using your voice to help you with your day. Ask Google to play your favorite song, navigate home, or respond to a text hands-free. Just say, “Hey Google” or long press the voice control button on your steering wheel.

3/3 USB-C + USB-A

USB-C + USB-A ports up front let passengers connect and charge their compatible smartphones and other gadgets.

SURROUNDS YOU IN CONFIDENCE

Technologies to help keep you safe and unlock the joy of driving.

NISSAN ADVANCED SAFETY FEATURES

DRIVER ASSIST TECHNOLOGIES

1/5 INTELLIGENT EMERGENCY BRAKING

Intelligent Emergency Braking can help save you from a potential frontal collision. It monitors your speed and distance between you and the car ahead and can let you know if you need to slow down. It can also automatically engage the brakes to help avoid a collision or lessen the severity of an impact.

2/5 INTELLIGENT FORWARD COLLISION WARNING

X-Terra is watching two vehicles ahead, even when you can’t see what’s going on up front. And when it detects sudden deceleration in front of you, it gives you a warning to slow down

3/5 BLIND SPOT WARNING

Blind Spot Warning keeps an eye on the blind spot area and gives you a heads-up if it detects a vehicle hiding there.

4/5 LANE DEPARTURE WARNING

Lane Departure Warning helps make sure you change lanes only when you mean to. If the system detects that you’re drifting over, it’ll warn you.

5/5 REAR CROSS TRAFFIC ALERT

When you’re backing out of a space, Rear Cross Traffic Alert watches around the rear of your X-Terra, warning you about cars it detects creeping up on you from either side.

DRIVER ASSIST TECHNOLOGIES

1/3 AROUND VIEW MONITOR

The Intelligent Around View Monitor has four cameras that give you a 360° birds-eye view of your vehicle so you can squeeze through city streets or nab a tough parking spot.

2/3 INTELLIGENT DRIVER ALERTNESS

Intelligent Driver Alertness monitors your steering patterns and if it detects you might need a break, it alerts you with a chime and a coffee cup icon on your display.

3/3 INTELLIGENT AUTO HEADLIGHTS

Nissan’s Intelligent Auto Headlights react to light conditions outside, automatically turning your lights on and off when needed. They also react to your windshield wipers, automatically turning on after just four swipes.

X-TERRA PERFORMANCE

OFF-ROAD PERFORMANCE

With X-Terra, the family outing takes on a whole new meaning. Rugged engineering, plus advanced tech, lets you do things on a different scale.

OFF-ROAD HARDWARE

1/3 BODY FRAME

X-Terra is designed on a reinforced, fully boxed, full-length ladder frame for exceptional capability and durability.

2/3 SUSPENSION

The reinforced double-wishbone front suspension has been tuned for responsive, confident handling, while the rear 5-link suspension is tuned for a comfortable ride.

3/3 GROUND CLEARANCE

Impressive angles of approach (32.3°) and departure (26.6°), along with 255cm of clearance, help give the X-Terra the capability to travel where other vehicles can’t.

REVOLUTIONISING OFF-ROAD

1/4 HILL START ASSIST

When starting on an incline, hill start assist can help keep you from rolling backwards. It simply holds the brakes for a few seconds so you can switch from brake pedal to accelerator with confidence.

2/4 HILL DESCENT CONTROL

When driving down hilly terrain, hill descent control automatically maintains a steady speed and brake pressure to help you safely get back down a steep grade.

3/4 OFF-ROAD, AROUND VIEW MONITOR

In 4LO, the Intelligent Around View Monitor gives you a clear view of the surrounding terrain, so you can avoid large obstacles and see exactly where to place your front wheels for a clear path ahead.

4/4 OFF-ROAD METER

Stay informed about your off-road performance. Longitudinal and lateral inclination, steering, 4x4 power, and more right on the Advanced Drive-Assist Display.
