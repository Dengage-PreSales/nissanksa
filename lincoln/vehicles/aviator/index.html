FAQs

 

What is the price of the Lincoln Aviator in Saudi Arabia?

The Lincoln Aviator is priced starting at SAR 317,745 in Saudi Arabia. Final pricing may vary depending on trim level, features, and optional packages.

What are the key features of the Lincoln Aviator?

Key features include premium interior materials, advanced technology, powerful performance, spacious seating, Lincoln Co-Pilot360™ 2.1 driver-assist systems, and a refined, quiet ride.

How does the Lincoln Aviator compare to other luxury SUVs?

The Aviator stands out with its commanding performance, elegant design, quiet interior, and advanced safety technologies - offering a more refined and premium experience than many competing luxury SUVs.

Where can I buy the Lincoln Aviator in Saudi Arabia?
The Aviator is available at Lincoln Al Naghi authorized showrooms across Saudi Arabia. Customers can visit the nearest location to explore the model and schedule a test drive.
What engine options are available for the Lincoln Aviator?

The Aviator offers a powerful engine designed to deliver smooth acceleration, strong torque, and a confident driving experience suitable for both city roads and long-distance travel.

Does the Lincoln Aviator offer advanced safety features?

Yes. The Aviator includes a full suite of advanced safety and driver-assistance technologies, enhancing protection, awareness, and driver confidence on every journey.
