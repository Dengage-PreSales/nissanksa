Channel copy for the Nissan demo

Every message the Monday journeys and rehearsals need. Each field has its own copy button, matching the panel composer's separate Title and Body inputs. The copy is deliberately model neutral, because a journey fires for whichever car the person chose; production inserts the name and the model from the contact and the event, and these bodies read correctly without them. All of it is pre-purchase; nothing here services, renews or reminds an owner. SMS bodies stay inside one segment and carry the STOP opt-out.
