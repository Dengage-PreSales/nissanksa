Verification console

Reads the demo's own state out of Dengage. Nothing here writes anything, and this page deliberately loads no part of the demo: a verification tool that fires its own page view would appear in the numbers it is reporting.

1. What can message today

Each moment needs an email content and a push content in the panel. A moment reading no email reaches the drawer and the push only, which is a working demo and not a broken one. This is the fastest way to confirm a content id you have just set actually took.

2. Are events landing in the tables

Take a baseline, go and use the demo, come back and read again. Two things to know before you trust the answer. Storage lags by roughly two minutes, so a reading taken straight after a click shows nothing and means nothing. And the account is shared, so a count that moved is not proof it was your event, while a count that did not move is proof it was not.

3. The walk that touches every table

  1. Take the baseline above, then open the demo in another tab.
  2. Home: search for patrol, then press a heart on any car. page_view_events, search_events, wishlist_events
  3. A model page: press Watch the price. wishlist_events
  4. Build and reserve: choose a grade, choose a different one, then reserve it and fill the form in. shopping_cart_events add, remove, begin_checkout, then order_events
  5. Compare two cars, then answer all three questions on Find your Nissan. ni_lead_events: compare, chooser with the purchase horizon
  6. My Showroom: press Not this one on the build. shopping_cart_events delete_cart
  7. Dealer cockpit: pick DPS-1, log a walk in, then cancel the test drive. ni_lead_events, and order_events reversing the booking
  8. Wait two minutes, then press Read again.

Every table should have moved. If one has not, the demo did not write it, and ?debug=1 on any page shows what that page actually sent and to which table.
